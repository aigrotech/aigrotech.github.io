FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it work without internet on site? [[I plus]]

Yes. Besides Wi-Fi, the board can connect over SIM-based mobile data; that route is used on land without internet.

Do I need to replace my tank? [[I plus]]

In our installations the level sensor was fitted on top of the existing tank. We assess your own tank together during the site survey.

Which alarms are available? [[I plus]]

Low-level, turbidity and device connection alarms. Alarm history is kept on the dashboard.

Can I control the pump remotely? [[I plus]]

Yes. The pump can be switched on and off from the dashboard via the relay on the controller board.
