The problem

A fixed threshold only sees the problem after it happens.

Manual or fixed-threshold dosing reacts too late. Action comes once values leave the band; fertiliser and water are wasted and roots get stressed.

AIgro reverses this loop: it forecasts where pH and EC are heading and chooses the smallest dose needed to hold the band.

How it works

Measure, forecast, verify, dose

The loop is closed: after every dose the system measures again and bases its next decision on the result.

Safe autonomy

AI recommends, the safety layer decides.

The AI never drives the pump directly. Every dosing command passes through an independent safety layer on the controller board.

[[I shield]]

Safety envelope

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI run the pump directly? [[I plus]]

No. The AI recommends a dose; the command does not reach the pump without passing the independent safety layer on the controller board.

What happens if the internet goes down? [[I plus]]

The system keeps running locally. Where there is no internet, the board can also connect over SIM-based mobile data.

What is measured? [[I plus]]

pH, EC, water temperature and tank level. Dosing runs on three channels: Nutrient A, Nutrient B and pH adjuster.

Are the savings figures guaranteed? [[I plus]]

No. They are literature-based targets. In a pilot they are measured in your greenhouse and reported exactly as they turn out.

How does a pilot start? [[I plus]]

The first step is to visit your greenhouse and choose the right line together. You can request a meeting on the contact page.
