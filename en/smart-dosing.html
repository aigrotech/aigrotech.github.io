Smart Nutrient Dosing

Safe autonomous dosing for soilless greenhouses that forecasts pH and EC and explains its decisions.

The problem

A fixed threshold only sees the problem after it happens.

Manual or fixed-threshold dosing reacts too late. Action comes once values leave the band; fertiliser and water are wasted and roots get stressed.

AIgro reverses this loop: it forecasts where pH and EC are heading and chooses the smallest dose needed to hold the band.

How it works

Measure, forecast, verify, dose

The loop is closed: after every dose the system measures again and bases its next decision on the result.

  • 01 · MeasurepH, EC, water temperature and tank level
  • 02 · ForecastAI predicts where pH/EC are heading and picks the smallest dose
  • 03 · VerifyThe controller board checks every command against safety limits
  • 04 · Dose3 channels: Nutrient A · Nutrient B · pH adjuster

Safe autonomy

AI recommends, the safety layer decides.

AI recommends the dose and the controller board applies it within safety limits. The AI never drives the pump directly; every command passes through an independent safety layer.

Safety envelope

Commands are checked against limits defined on the board; a command that exceeds them is not executed.

Works even offline

If the connection drops, the system keeps running locally.

Explainable decisions

The dashboard logs every dose by channel and amount and explains why it was given in plain language.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI run the pump directly?

No. The AI recommends a dose; the command does not reach the pump without passing the independent safety layer on the controller board.

What happens if the internet goes down?

The system keeps running locally. Where there is no internet, the board can also connect over SIM-based mobile data.

What is measured?

pH, EC, water temperature and tank level. Dosing runs on three channels: Nutrient A, Nutrient B and pH adjuster.

Are the savings figures guaranteed?

No. They are literature-based targets. After installation they are measured in your greenhouse and reported exactly as they turn out.

How does installation start?

The first step is to visit your greenhouse and choose the right line together. You can request a meeting on the contact page.
