FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it work without internet on site?

Yes. Besides Wi-Fi, the board can connect over SIM-based mobile data; that route is used on land without internet.

Do I need to replace my tank?

In our field installations the level sensor was fitted on top of the existing tank. We assess your own tank together during the site survey.

Which alarms are available?

Low-level, turbidity and device connection alarms. Alarm history is kept on the dashboard.

Can I control the pump remotely?

Yes. The pump can be switched on and off from the dashboard via the relay on the controller board.
